What this figure shows
- Overall SAT-decision accuracy vs number of variables (n) under examples-only prompting (k=3).
- Two panels (left/right in the PDF):
  left: OpenAI gpt-5.2-pro (think=high): Non-Horn compares Compact CNF vs Natural Language CNF (n<=50),
        while Horn Compact CNF is included and remains at 1.00 through n=100.
  right: OpenAI gpt-5.2 (think=none): Horn vs Non-Horn overlay for Compact CNF and Natural Language CNF (n<=50),
         with If-then CNF shown on Horn only.

Axis spacing note (important)
- The x-axis is treated as a discrete set of n values for readability (spacing is not linear in n).
- Tick layout: 10->0, 20->0.7, 30->1.4, 40->2.1, 50->2.8, 60->3.3, 80->3.7, 100->4
- x-scale unification: panels share the same x-position mapping for n=10..50; left panel additionally shows n in {60,80,100}.
- xlim: left panel [-0.2,4.2] ; right panel [-0.14,2.94] (x-position units)
